A slower morning on Lake Bunyonyi

Pearl & Path Journal · 18 Oct 2026 · 5 min read

Morning arrives gently on Lake Bunyonyi. Mist drifts above the water, the islands emerge in layers, and the hills catch the first light.

Set aside time to take in the view, explore the shoreline, and enjoy the quiet rhythm of the highlands.
