WATERFALLS · MOUNT ELGON

Where Sipi Falls sings through the hills.

Pearl & Path Journal · 18 Oct 2026 · 5 min read

In the foothills of Mount Elgon, the landscape opens into green valleys, coffee gardens and ridges that seem to roll towards the horizon. Then comes the sound of water: Sipi Falls spilling down the rock face in a bright, silver ribbon.

Follow the winding paths through the hills and the scenery changes with every turn. Cool mist drifts through the air near the falls, while far below, the valley stretches into a patchwork of farms and forest. After the walk, pause to take it all in—the rushing water, the fresh mountain air and the wide view beyond.

Sipi invites you to slow your steps and let the journey be part of the destination. Here, a simple walk becomes a collection of moments: green hills beneath an open sky, water echoing through the valley and a view that stays with you long after you leave.
