RIVERS · EAST AFRICA

Where the Nile begins its journey

Pearl & Path Journal · 18 Oct 2026 · 5 min read

In Jinja, the river moves through a landscape of green banks, open skies and shifting light. One moment, the water glimmers in the sun; the next, clouds pass overhead and turn the view silver.

Along the shoreline, the sound of rushing water mingles with birdsong and the quiet rhythm of daily life. Take a boat onto the river, pause to watch the current, or simply find a place to sit and let the scenery unfold around you.

The Source of the Nile is more than a landmark. It is a beautiful place to slow down, look out across the water and feel the pull of a journey beginning.
