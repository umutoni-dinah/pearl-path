Into the forest: a Bwindi gorilla trek

Pearl & Path Journal · 18 Oct 2026 · 5 min read

The Bwindi Impenetrable Forest is a place of wonder. Towering trees, thick undergrowth, and the calls of wildlife create an immersive experience for those who venture into its depths.

Trekking through the forest to observe mountain gorillas is a once-in-a-lifetime experience. The guides lead visitors through the dense foliage, sharing knowledge about the ecosystem and the conservation efforts in place to protect these magnificent creatures.
