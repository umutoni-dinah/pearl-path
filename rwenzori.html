MOUNTAINS · RWENZORI

Where the Rwenzoris meet the clouds.

Pearl & Path Journal · 18 Oct 2026 · 5 min read

The Rwenzori Mountains rise into drifting cloud, their green slopes unfolding one ridge at a time. Ferns frame the trail, valleys disappear into mist, and the changing light gives the landscape a new mood with every passing cloud.

A walk here invites you to notice the small things: cool air on the climb, water winding through the valley and the hush that settles when the clouds draw close. Higher up, the landscape feels both rugged and delicate, shaped by weather and time.

The Rwenzoris reward a slower pace. Pause to look back across the mountain folds, listen to the forest and take in the view. For a moment, the world feels far away—and the mountains hold your attention completely.
