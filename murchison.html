RIVERS · MURCHISON FALLS

Where the Nile thunders through Murchison Falls

Pearl & Path Journal · 18 Oct 2026 · 5 min read

The Nile gathers speed through a green landscape, then rushes into a narrow cleft in the rock. At Murchison Falls, water surges through the gap and bursts into a cloud of spray, filling the air with sound and mist.

From the riverbank, the scene feels alive with movement: white water against dark stone, green vegetation along the cliffs and sunlight catching the spray. Take a moment to watch the river press forward. The falls are powerful, dramatic and impossible to hurry past.

Here, the scenery tells its story through contrast—the stillness of the surrounding landscape beside the force of the water. It is a place to pause, breathe in the cool air and remember the feeling of standing close to something wild and immense.
